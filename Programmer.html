Meet Our Website Creator

Behind this website is a heart devoted to serving God and building this ministry, one page at a time.

Liveni Matu

Ministry Volunteer · Website Builder

A 19-year-old from East London with a passion for building digital experiences that serve God's kingdom. Through a spiritual revival, I discovered my calling to use my skills in web development to create spaces where others can find hope and encouragement. I believe that every line of code I write is an act of service to this ministry and to everyone who visits this website.

My Heart for This Ministry

My name is Liveni. I'm 19 years old, born and raised right here in East London. I serve as a Ministry Volunteer at Christ Life Restoration Ministries, and it's been my joy to design and build this very website for our church family.

Why I Serve

A Willing Heart

This website exists because of a revival that changed me. It gave me the courage to embrace who I truly am and showed me just how much power and talent had been hidden inside me all along. I decided to take that gift and use it, pouring my skills as a developer into building something that could serve this ministry and everyone who visits it.

What I Do

Building With Purpose

I took on the design and development of this website myself — from the layout to the colours to every page you see. My goal was to create a space where people could find hope, encouragement, and everything they need to know about our church. Colossians 3:23 quotes, "Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters." This verse guides my approach to every aspect of the website's creation.
