About Us

Who we are, what we believe, and why we exist — to see lives restored through Jesus Christ.

EST. 2018

Welcome to Christ Life Restoration Ministries

Christ Life Restoration Ministries started in 2018 out of a simple desire — share the love of Jesus and bring real hope and healing to people's lives.

Everyone has value and purpose in God's plan, no matter what season they're walking through. There's hope through Christ.

We want to be a place where people can worship, grow in faith, receive prayer, and serve others.

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel, teach God's Word, and help people and families find real restoration.

We want to reach people with the message of salvation, strengthen believers, and support those going through hard seasons.

Our Vision

Transformed lives, restored families, and a community passionate about following Jesus.

A church family where people find their purpose and carry it into their homes and communities.

Our Core Values

✝️ Faith

Living by faith in God and His Word, every day.

❤️ Love

Showing Christ's love by caring for people and our community.

🙏 Prayer

Prayer runs through everything we do here.

📖 God's Word

Building our lives on the truth of Scripture.

🕊️ Restoration

God restores hope, heals hearts, and gives new beginnings.

🤝 Fellowship

We grow stronger worshiping, praying, and serving together.

What We Believe

  • One God, Creator of heaven and earth.
  • Jesus Christ, the Son of God and Savior of the world.
  • Salvation through faith in Jesus Christ.
  • The Holy Spirit works in the lives of God's people.
  • The Bible is God's Word and foundational to Christian life.
  • The power of prayer, worship, fellowship, and service.
  • God gives hope, purpose, and restoration.
  • We're called to love God and love others.
